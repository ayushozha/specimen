# Storylines

Recommended: **A**, because it follows one real section from the page into the tools people use, which is the product's promise in one take.

## A · One section, all the way (built)

Device: one object across apps. Form: object-to-object chain.
The iPhone hero on apple.com (the person picked the site). Specimen's picker outlines it, captures it with its motion and exact colours, and the same section is carried into Figma, Framer and a coding agent's DESIGN.md. The viewer feels the gap between a flat screenshot and a capture they can keep working from.

- First two seconds: apple.com's iPhone hero; a large cursor moves over the headline and the picker's outline and size label snap onto it.
- Beats: hook (picker on the card) → tension (the screenshot freezes the card flat) → reveal (the side panel opens: Specimen) → proof (pick · capture at 3 widths · motion · colours) → payoff (Export: Figma, Framer, Code agent; DESIGN.md pours out) → claim → end card.
- Look: the site's own paper ground `#fffaf5`, ink `#2b2849`, accent `#64509b` (read by Specimen from its own site), Manrope.
- Sound: soft UI clicks on real clicks, ticks on the checklist, whooshes on the three world changes, one riser into the payoff. Music bed to add once a track source is available.

## B · The screenshot that fails

Device: life before and after. Form: split screen to one.
Left, someone screenshots a site and pastes it into a design tool: flat, the motion gone, a guessed hex. Right, the same page through Specimen: layers, clips, the exact `oklch` value. The split collapses into the Specimen side.

- First two seconds: two identical hero sections side by side; the left one freezes with a camera-shutter flash.
- Beats: hook → the left side keeps failing → collapse into the right → proof → end card.
- Look: same brand ground; the "old way" desaturated to 30%.
- Sound: a shutter, then the left side drops to near silence while the right side carries the bed.

## C · Copy everything (bold)

Device: type carries the argument. Form: cut on the beat.
The film is the DESIGN.md itself: giant kinetic headings from the real spec (Colours, Type, Motion, Stack) cut on the beat, each one opening onto the real capture it describes.

- First two seconds: `# Specimen — design spec` types in at 9% of frame height and the page scrolls under it.
- Beats: spec heading → the capture it names, ×5 → Copy everything → end card.
- Look: dark ink ground `#211d3d` with paper type, accent on one word per heading.
- Sound: a 120 BPM pulse; key ticks on each heading.
